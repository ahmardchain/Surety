Social Money · on Sputnik DAO

A group that saves together lends to its own members.

Members stand behind each loan instead of collateral. The money and the approvals stay in the group's Sputnik DAO. Surety adds the loan rules: guarantor pledges, repayments back to the treasury, a one-call claim on default, and borrowing limits earned by repaying.
